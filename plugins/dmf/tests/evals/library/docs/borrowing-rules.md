# Borrowing Rules

A member may borrow a copy when all of these hold:

- The member exists and is not suspended.
  A suspension ends on its end date.
- The copy exists, is not reference-only, and is not already on loan.
- The member holds fewer than 5 loans, or fewer than 3 for a junior member.
- The member owes less than 10.00 in unpaid fines.

A loan lasts 21 days, or 7 days for a new release.
When the app asks for a loan that breaks a rule, it tells the member which rule.
When a loan breaks several rules, the app names the first one, in the order above.
